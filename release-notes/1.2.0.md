- New Settings page rows: `StartupSetting`, `ThemeSetting` and `LanguageSetting` give your Settings page the standard "Start with Windows" switch, the "App theme" choice and the "Language" picker, with the kit's translated labels. The startup switch shows when the user (in Windows Settings) or a policy has decided instead of your app. Hook each row's event to save the choice in your settings; the README's "Settings page rows" section shows how.
- Five more shared labels now come from the kit: On, Off, and the three notes that explain a disabled startup switch. Remove your own copies when you switch to the rows.
- Nothing changes for your app until you adopt these: existing APIs and behaviour are as in 1.1.0.
