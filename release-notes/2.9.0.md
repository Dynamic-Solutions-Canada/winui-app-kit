- **Heroes re-render after the bump:** the kit now runs devkit v0.29.0, which sets a Store hero headline or subline up to 10 percent smaller when the largest size that fits would leave a single word alone on a line. If one of your heroes has such a line, `check:hero` fails after the bump until you re-render it with `scripts/devkit hero`. Review the changed images before committing; other heroes are unchanged.
- **Catch a kit workflow tag that drifts from the submodule:** add `external/winui-app-kit/scripts/check-kit-ref .` to your app's `validate`. It fails when a workflow's `uses:` line for one of the kit's reusable workflows names any ref other than `v` plus the kit submodule's version, the same mismatch the Store workflow otherwise reports only once a release tag is already publishing.
