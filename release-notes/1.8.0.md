- New shared Microsoft Store publishing: call the kit's reusable `store-publish.yml` from your own workflow and delete your copy. A tag uploads a draft with your release notes, and a manual run on the same tag submits it after checking it against the tag. Repairing a draft and replacing a canceled or failed submission work as before, and a dry run checks everything without touching the Store. Copy the caller from `tests/store/callers/store-publish.yml`; the README's "Publishing to the Microsoft Store" section explains the inputs.
- New shared credential reminder: call the kit's `credential-expiry.yml` on a schedule, passing your `STORE_SECRET_EXPIRES` variable, and it fails 60 days before the Store secret expires.
- Keep the workflow's `uses:` tag equal to your kit submodule pin: the Store workflow stops at its first step if they differ.
- `SettingsShell.Select` now works before the window has loaded, so a tray "Settings" entry or the settings reset notice can open the window straight on a page. You can drop any wait-for-loaded ordering around it.
- The built-in Settings item's tooltip and screen-reader name now follow your app's language too, not only its label.
