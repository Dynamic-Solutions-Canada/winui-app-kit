- **Localized Store screenshots:** a screenshot entry in `store-assets/manifest.json` can now put `{locale}` in both `file` and `source` (for example `screenshots/{locale}/01-home-dark.png` and `raw/microsoft/{locale}/01-home-dark.png`). `render:screenshots` then crops one image per listing locale with its own record in `listings.json`, from that locale's capture, so Windows, the app and its sample data appear in the listing's language. A `copyFrom` locale (en-CA, fr-CA) uploads its target's images. `check:screenshots` fails when a listing locale has no capture (there is no fallback to en-US), when `{locale}` is in only one of `file` and `source`, and when a locale folder holds a PNG the manifest doesn't produce, including one for a dropped locale. The Store workflow's `screenshots` input uploads each locale's own set, in manifest order, with the hero first. Manifests without `{locale}` work as before, and both kinds of entry can be mixed, so you can move one screenshot at a time. See "Localized screenshots" in the README.
- **Captures at 200% display scale:** captures may now be taken at 200% (3840 x 2160 for the usual 1920 x 1080 desktop) as well as 100%. Every crop rule (work area, window, margin, brightness) is applied in logical pixels, so the crop lands where a 100% capture's would, and the result is reduced to the Store size by averaging each 2 x 2 block: the same layout and size, with smoother text. 100% captures are cropped exactly as before. Other scales fail the check.
- **devkit v0.30.0:** the kit now runs devkit v0.30.0. A hero's `source` may contain `{locale}`, and `check:hero` fails for a listing locale without its own capture (a plain `scripts/devkit hero` run previews it from the en-US capture). `hero.crop` is in logical pixels, which is unchanged at 100%. Headlines keep more lone words with their neighbours, shrinking up to 15 percent.
- **Heroes re-render after the bump:** `check:hero` fails until you re-render with `scripts/devkit hero` and commit, for 16 heroes in seven apps: DisplaySwitchr (de-DE), ImmichDrive (ja-JP), ChatMixr (es-ES, pt-BR), Persistent (en-US, es-ES, de-DE, pt-BR), Little Launcher (es-ES, pt-BR), Tray Control for WSL (es-ES, fr-FR, pt-BR) and Repilot (fr-FR, de-DE, pt-BR). Review the changed images before committing.
