- New `TrayIconHost` for apps with more than one tray icon: add an icon per item with `Add`, each with its own clicks, image, tooltip and stable GUID, and hide or show any of them with `IsVisible`. All icons come back after Explorer restarts. The README's "Several tray icons" section shows how.
- `TrayIconHost.GuidFromName` gives each icon a GUID that never changes, so Windows keeps the user's show-or-hide choice for it.
- Single-icon apps change nothing: `new TrayIcon(...)` works as before, and now also has `IsVisible`.
