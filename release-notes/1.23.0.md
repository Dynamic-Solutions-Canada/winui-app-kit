- Store screenshots are now real desktop captures, cropped. `render-screenshots.py` takes the test VM's full desktop (captured with windows-build-tools 73b96b8 or later: dark, 1920 x 1080 at 100%, window effects on, the dark wallpaper, the window centred) and crops it to 1366 x 768 around your window, or the bottom-right corner with the taskbar for a tray flyout (`"crop": "tray"`). Nothing is composited, shaved or scaled.
- Changed: the script no longer takes `--wallpaper`, and every raw capture needs the `.json` the capture helper writes beside it. Recapture your screenshots with the helper; captures from the old renderer no longer pass.
- `check:screenshots` also fails a capture that was scaled, light, without window effects or on another wallpaper, a screenshot whose app window is bright (an app set to its own light theme), and a screenshot not written by this renderer. See "Store screenshots" in the README.
